# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions
about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated
in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through
the email address on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/OWNER).
Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that
break these rules, and may block people from the project temporarily or
permanently.
